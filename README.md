Company Overview
Blueprint Group Inc. is a Saint Lucian construction and hardware company based in Beausejour, Gros Islet. The group has operated since at least 2011, with its construction division formally registered in 2012. The company caters to high-end home renovations and new hotel construction on the island.

The group operates as a unified brand under Blueprint Group Inc., encompassing four core divisions:

Blueprint Construction
Blueprint Hardware
Blueprint Rentals
Blueprint Management

Divisions
Blueprint Construction
A premier general construction company in Saint Lucia. Provides residential and small-to-medium commercial construction services, including new building construction, home renovations, remodeling, structural repairs, and general contracting work. The division also handles finishing works such as masonry and property improvement projects.

A key differentiator: Blueprint Construction manufactures its own construction blocks.

Blueprint Hardware
A retail hardware store located in Beausejour, Gros Islet, serving contractors, builders, and homeowners. Stocks construction materials and supplies. This division is the public-facing retail arm of the group.

Blueprint Rentals
Rental services for construction equipment, including trucks, tractors, backhoes, and other machinery. Serves contractors and project managers who need equipment without purchasing it outright.

Blueprint Management
The project management and coordination division of the group. Handles oversight and management of construction projects and possibly broader business operations within the group.
